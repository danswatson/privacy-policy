3. SMS Messaging

If you provide your phone number, you may receive SMS messages related to appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders, and responses to your inquiries.

We may use automated systems, including AI, to process and respond to messages in order to provide timely and efficient communication.

  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Message frequency may vary
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP
  • You can reply HELP for assistance
We do not use your phone number for marketing purposes without your consent, and we do not sell your personal information.
